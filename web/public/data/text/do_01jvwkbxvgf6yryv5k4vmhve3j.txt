Kommentarer (f.eks. informasjon om feltarbeidet innebærer strømmålere, eller om det er gjort korrigeringer av feltplanen under feltarbeidet ol.):  
Station placement based on standard OLEX bathymetry data (not site-specific multibeam), bottom current meter data (36 m) (Holen, 2021) as outflow is on the 
seabed and particle dispersion modelling for alternative 1 (Espenes & Djuve, 2021). 
Current flow measurements show main flow direction of 210-225° & 60-75°. Dispersion model results show highest particle deposition in SW-SSW direction from the 
outlet (see Fig1 in vedlegg). C1-C3 were thus placed in SSW direction following main current flow direction indicated by current meter measurements. C2 is thereby 
300 m from the outlet and the deepest station. An alternative C2 (C2alt) was placed in NE direction. The particle deposition distribution outlined in Fig 1 indicates a 
reduced spreading area in this direction and thus C2alt was placed at 250 m from the outlet. Cref was placed at the opposite site of the bay at a similar depth to the 
remaining stations.
