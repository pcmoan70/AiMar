1 AREA OF USE  
This SOP is for the use of  test organisms in the climate rooms for GLP tests and non GLP 
tests.  
2 PRINCIPLES  
Chlorination of waste water from holding tanks and test systems containing the fish listed 
in Table 1, are performed in order to ensure that disease carrying bacteria or virus are not 
spread and to kill any potential parasites  before waste water is disposed. 
 
To prevent possible spread of potentially disease carrier organisms like virus, bacteria and 
parasites all waste water from holding tanks and/or test system in which organisms 
included in Table 1 are kept, should be treated in accordance with this procedure. All the 
species in Table 1 are non-native species in the Norwegian environment and may therefore 
introduce new parasites and diseases.  
 
Table 1. Species that may carry risk of disease in the form of parasites, virus or bacteria

 6 INSTRUMENTS AND EQUIPMENT 
Climate rooms R1, R2, R3 and R4 are used for GLP purposes and non GLP purposes. 
Climate room R2 is used exclusively for culturing purposes, while R3 and R4 are used for 
testing. Climate room R1 can be used for both culturing and testing.  
 
7 PROCEDURE  
 
As part of the planning of introduce any of test organisms indicated in Table 1, dedicated 
25 liter cannisters are labeled as “ Species Waste” and placed in relevant climate room. All 
water that has been in contact with said species is filled into the dedicated 25 liter cannister.  
 
Chlorination 
The aim of the chlorination is to achieve a level of 35 mg Cl/L of free chlorine in the waste water. 
Adding 0.875 ml Klorin to 1 liter of water give 35 mg Cl/L (Loncarovic, 2017) 
 
When cannister is approximately full (20-25 liter) add 20 ml Klorin, put on lid and shake. Wait for 
at least 30 minutes before discharging into sink. Date of chlorination is noted directly on label of 
cannister.
