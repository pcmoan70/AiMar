Procedure – Medical anesthesia or euthanasia of fish

1 AREA OF USE 
This procedure is specific for fish kept as part of the NIVA aquaculture activity at Ø94 
2 PRINCIPLES  
In case fish is planned to undergo planned procedures that will involve short term pain or stress, then 
anesthesia should be applied. Also, procedure like vaccination, tag marking, measurement of length 
or weighing requires anesthesia. Personnel applying this procedure should have documented animal 
welfare course and training in the procedure prior to applying it.  
A procedure of humane killing of fish through euthanasia, is also described. 
3 RESPONSIBILITY  
As per SOP responsible list (GLP 007 B001).  
4 DEFINITIONS  
PL=Project leader, PMSK=Personnel with special control responsibility 
5 CHEMICALS AND SOLUTIONS  
Benzoac® Vet. (ACD Pharmaceuticals or Sigma Aldrich) supplied by NIVA veterinary contact person.  
 6 INSTRUMENTS AND EQUIPMENT  
-Holding tank for fish to be sedated or euthanized 
7 PROCEDURE  
7.1 Sedation of fish 
-Choose a holding tank that will be just enough to hold all the fish that needs to be treated. Measure 
up exactly the amount of water needed and add Benzoac® vet. amount that will give active 
ingredient (ai) of 30-40 mg ai/L, equal to 0.15-0.20 ml Benzoac® vet./liter. Stir water prior to adding 
fish. Fish should not be fed 48 hours prior to sedation. 
-Test the treatment solution with 2 fish. Sedation should be achieved within 2-5 minutes. If no effect, 
remove fish, then add 20 % more of Benzoac® vet. and test again. The fish should be carefully 
monitored in order to avoid overtreatment. Check especially gill movement, non-normal gill 
movement indicate too high treatment concentration. 
-Fish is added individually according to the test procedure that the fish is about to undergo. 
Maximum period in treatment tank is 15 minutes.  
-Place fish in clean well aerated water after commencing procedure.  
 
7.1 Killing of fish 
-Prepare a suitable container with known volume of water. Add Benzoac® vet. to get an active 
ingredient of 60-80 mg ai/L, equal to 0.3-0.4 ml Benzoac® vet./liter. Add fish to treatment container
